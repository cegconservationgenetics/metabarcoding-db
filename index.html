These curated databases were developed to support DNA metabarcoding analyses by providing taxonomically annotated reference sequences associated with individual metabarcoding primer regions. This website is an interactive viewer for exploring their content.

Interpretation

“Reference sequence present” means only:

A corresponding reference sequence is present in the curated database.

It does not mean:

  • × The species will be amplified by the primer.
  • × The species was detected in samples.
  • × The species occurs at the study site.
  • × The species contributed to otter diet.
  • × The primer is specific to that species.
The number of reference sequences must not be interpreted as ecological abundance.
